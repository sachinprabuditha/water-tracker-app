# Privacy Policy for H2Go

_Last updated: [DATE]_

H2Go ("the app") is developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information the app stores

- **Water log and settings**: the number of glasses you log each day, your daily goal, glass size, and reminder times. This is stored on your device.
- **Cloud backup (optional)**: if cloud backup is enabled in Settings, the water log and settings are also saved to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under an **anonymous identifier** generated by Firebase Authentication. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account details.

## Notifications

The app uses local notifications on your device to remind you to drink water. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's system settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device and do not involve sending your data anywhere.

## How we use information

Only to provide the app's features: showing your progress and history, sending reminders, and backing up your data if you choose.

## Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. Data stored in Firebase is processed by Google LLC as our service provider. See Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access rules allow each anonymous user to read and write only their own data.

## Your choices and deletion

- Turn off **Cloud backup** in Settings to delete your cloud copy and the anonymous account associated with it.
- Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
- You can also contact us at the address below to request deletion.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Health disclaimer

H2Go is a general wellness tool. It does not provide medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional about your hydration needs.
